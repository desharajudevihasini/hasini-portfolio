THE SPARK

Why this one?

Outdoor safety is not only about getting a location after something goes wrong. We wanted to explore how a small wearable could bring position, movement and a way to call for help together.

THE BUILD

How it works

An ESP32 safety-wearable experiment combining GNSS, heart-rate sensing, motion input, OLED readout, a buzzer and a physical SOS. The design explores BLE and location-aware safety behavior.

ESP32 · GNSS · MAX30102 · MPU6050 · BLE

THE COMPONENTS

Inside the project

  • GNSS location and activity inputs
  • Physical SOS action and local alerting
  • Wearable sensing and phone-connectivity experiments
